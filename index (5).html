12-Week Development Plan
2-3 sessions per week · 60-75 mins · Ball mastery every session. Game time always last.
Every Session · 75 mins
The 6 Phases
Home Training — Secret Weapon
10-15 mins daily · Just a ball
· Ball mastery routine (toe taps, sole rolls, inside-outside)
· Juggling — both feet, count your record
· Wall passing — right foot pass, left foot control
Ronaldo and Neymar built their foundations doing exactly this. Every day. With just a ball and a wall.
Progress Checks · Every 2-3 Weeks
⚽
Juggling count — both feet. Record number. Beat it each check.
🆚
1v1 success rate — how many times does the player beat the defender?
🦶
Weak foot usage — is the player using it without being told?
🎯
First touch quality — does it set up the next action or create pressure?
